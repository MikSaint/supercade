adult.glb = "Body Male - Realistic"; kid5.glb = "Body Female - Stylized". Decimated, textures removed and re-centred
(feet at the origin) for the Supercade viewer. The viewer scales each one to the
height it represents (170 cm, 110 cm). The 8-year-old (128 cm) is the adult mesh
with a larger head and a slimmer body, made in the viewer.
